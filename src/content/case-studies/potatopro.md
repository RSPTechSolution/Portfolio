## Problem

The global potato industry needed one place for news, a business directory, company pages and inquiries, with search that stays fast as content grows.

## My role

Worked on both frontend and backend: gathered requirements with the client, coordinated with the design team on UI/UX, and delivered features under tight timelines.

## Architecture

Drupal 10 with multiple custom modules for business workflows: inquiry workflows, company pages and governance automation. Search API on OpenSearch/Solr powers enterprise search. API integrations connect Drupal with AI services and external enterprise platforms. Redis and the Drupal Cache API keep pages fast.

## Stack

Drupal 10, PHP, Search API, Solr/OpenSearch, Redis, Twig, Composer, Drush, MySQL, JavaScript and jQuery.

## Outcome

Live at potatopro.com. The project received a special award and appreciation after completion.
